Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need cybersecurity if I just have a standard website?

Yes. Automated bots crawl the web 24/7 looking for vulnerabilities in plugins, forms, and server configurations to inject malware or steal user data. No website is too small to be a target.

What happens if my site is currently compromised?

We offer rapid incident response. Our team will quarantine the server, remove the malicious code, restore a clean backup, and patch the entry vulnerability to prevent recurrence.

Are your cybersecurity services Dubai compliant with local laws?

Absolutely. We build security frameworks that help you remain compliant with the UAE Data Protection Law and industry-specific regulations.
